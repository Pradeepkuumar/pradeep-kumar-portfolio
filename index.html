01 · About me

From requirement to shipped app.

I'm Pradeep Kumar, a passionate Mobile Application Developer specializing in Flutter, Android, and iOS development. I enjoy building modern, reliable, and user-friendly applications that solve real-world business problems.

My primary expertise is Flutter & Dart, where I develop cross-platform applications for Android and iOS. I have experience with GetX, REST APIs, JSON, Firebase, local storage, responsive UI, navigation, image and document uploads, and third-party integrations.

Along with Flutter, I have experience in native Android development using Kotlin, working with Jetpack Compose, XML layouts, Data Binding, Retrofit, OkHttp, Moshi, Dagger Hilt, Firebase, and ML Kit. I also work with iOS development using Swift and SwiftUI, including API integration, Firebase, CocoaPods, Xcode, provisioning, and App Store deployment.

I have worked on applications in logistics, shipping, warehouse management, CRM, healthcare, and attendance — understanding complex business processes and transforming them into simple and efficient mobile experiences.
